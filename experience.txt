### POSTING FLOW - how often open roles appear

appear per period. Flow decides whether someone who checks back next
week finds anything new, and it sets refresh cadence.

  2026-08          423         40          7          6        103     579     13      2%

  Open roles: 1 in 2026-04, 13 in 2026-08 across 5 months on the board.
  CAUTION: the most recent month is almost always partial, and the
  oldest months are survivors - roles posted then and still open,
  not roles posted then. This is a snapshot, not a time series.
  A true flow rate needs repeat pulls and first_seen, not posted_at.
